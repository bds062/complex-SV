# Benchmarks

All reported predictions are out-of-fold at the genome level.

- `localization/`: interval-level labels, one-to-one matches, aggregate
  metrics, primary figures, and the localization training protocol.
- `chromosome/`: chromosome-level labels, held-out predictions, aggregate
  metrics, primary figures, and the chromosome training protocol.
- `ensemble/`: chromosome-level combinations of both methods.

Generated embeddings, per-fold training histories, exploratory plots, and
false-positive galleries are intentionally excluded from the publication
release.
